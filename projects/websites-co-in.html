Websites.co.in Teardown

I built a live site on a popular site builder, hit five real friction points, and traced each one to a probable cause and a fix.

Product teardownHands-on testing5 findings + a bonus2026

Context

I signed up on Websites.co.in and built a live site end to end: registration, business details, a page, and SEO setup. These are the five biggest friction points I hit, in the order I hit them.

What I found

1

The signup sidebar overlaps the form

What happens
On the first signup screen, the purple promotional sidebar covers part of the form, including the Full Name field and some of the dashboard preview text.

Why it matters
This is the first screen a new user sees. If a field looks blocked, some people will assume the form is broken and leave before they start, which is the worst place in the funnel to lose someone.

Fix
Cap the sidebar width or collapse it on smaller screens, and run a responsive check on the signup page first since it has the highest stakes.

2

Location detection fails even after a correct manual address

What happens
After I typed my address and PIN code manually, the form still showed "Unable to retrieve your location. Please enter your address manually."

Why it matters
The message tells you to do something you have just done, which is confusing and weakens trust in the rest of the form.

Fix
Separate the auto-detect failure from the manual-entry flow. If a valid address and PIN are present, hide the message or show "Location saved manually".

3

A duplicate dropdown renders on top of itself

What happens
On Edit Business Location, the country-code dropdown appears twice, stacked, and covers the phone and email fields until it is closed.

Why it matters
It is not only cosmetic. It blocks completing the form, and a new user may not realise they have to close it to reach the fields below.

Fix
This looks like one component being mounted twice. Worth auditing how that field is created across the location-edit flow.

4

The map pin lands in the wrong place

What happens
With the city and a postal code entered, "Locate on Map" placed the pin in a different, sparsely populated area more than 10 km away.

Why it matters
For a local business tool, a wrong pin undermines the main promise that nearby visitors can find you.

Fix
The lookup likely matches on city name alone. Weight the postal code more, or let the user drag the pin to correct it.

5

A confident SEO score on an empty page

What happens
Before I wrote any real content, with one default keyword, the SEO widget already showed "44 / FAIR" with a coloured progress ring.

Why it matters
A confident number on empty content is misleading. It gives false reassurance, and it erodes trust once the user realises almost nothing was scored.

Fix
Do not show a number until a minimum amount of content exists, and show "Add content to see your SEO score" until then.

BonusThe "Mail" button on my published site uses a plain mailto link with no fallback. If the device has no email client set up, it silently does nothing. A broken contact method with no feedback is a lost lead nobody sees.
