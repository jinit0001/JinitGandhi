The Lab

Small tools to try, not just read.

These two tools are small working demos I built to practise the ideas behind my projects. Everything runs in your browser, and nothing you type is saved or sent anywhere.

⌨️ Typing Mood Lab

KeyMood-AI reads emotion from how you type, compared with your own normal rhythm. This is a small, simple version of that idea. Type a few lines below, press Set my baseline, then type again in a different mood or speed.

Speed (WPM)0
Key hold0 ms
Gap between keys0 ms
Corrections0%
⌨️
Keep typing…Type about 15 keys or more and I will take a reading.

This is just for fun. It is a simple rule-based demo, not a medical or psychological tool, and real typing varies for many reasons.

🧭 Priority Matrix

A task that is due today is not always the most important one. Add tasks, mark each as urgent and/or important, and they sort into four boxes. Tap the buttons on a task to move it. The three starter tasks are the example from my screening assignment.

Do now

Urgent and important

Plan it

Important, not urgent

Handle quickly

Urgent, not important

Park it

Neither. Note it and ask for a deadline.
