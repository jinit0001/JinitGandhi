About me

A builder who likes finishing things.

I'm a final-year Computer Science Engineering student, specialising in Artificial Intelligence, at Parul Institute of Engineering and Technology in Vadodara. I'm originally from Mumbai.

I like taking problems that don't come with a clean spec, working out what is actually worth building, and then seeing it through.

I don't have formal industry experience yet. What I do have is the habit of building real things from start to end: team projects, product teardowns, and two simulations with real company scenarios. I'm also in the habit of slowing down to fix something properly instead of building around a problem.

This site is where I keep the write-ups: how I think, what I actually did, and what I would do differently next time.

How I like to work

  • Slow down to fix it properly. A quick patch on a shaky base costs more later.
  • Catch problems early. A cheap check on day one beats a big surprise on the last day.
  • Explain it plainly. If a result can't be understood, it can't be used.
